First work through the tutorial-development.asc tutorial to setup your morloc
environment. This tutorial is the most up-to-date material on morloc
startup. Then look in the morloc-project.github.io folder to find the official
morloc docs. Here find the sections 1-3 (Intro, Why Morloc, and Getting Started)
and work through it. Explore any code. Study the text. Explore beyond what the
documentation covers. In Getting Started, find any area where the official docs
are out of date or poorly explained. You're goal is to find bugs and
undocumented features in the morloc. Separate your report into two sections: 1)
issues in the documentation, inaccuracies, grammatical errors, missing
information, etc; 2) issues with the morloc langage such as bugs, poorly
designed features, or missing features.

Be sure you are using the very latest version of morloc.
