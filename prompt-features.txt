First work through the tutorial-development.asc tutorial to setup your morloc
environment. Then look in the morloc-project.github.io folder to find the
official morloc docs. Here look for the "Syntax and Features" section and work
through it. Try every feature. Study the text. Explore beyond what the
documentation covers. Look for exceptions and interactions between
features. You're goal is to find bugs and undocumented features in the
morloc. Separate your report into two sections: 1) issues in the documentation,
inaccuracies, grammatical errors, missing information, etc; 2) issues with the
morloc langage such as bugs, poorly designed features, or missing features.

Be sure you are using the very latest version of morloc.

Ignore any aparent errors in exit codes and DO NOT REPORT THEM, these are the
result of your environment stack (VM and morloc-manager). Morloc exit codes are
all correct.
